# Policy: Standard loan periods

Document ID: POL-01

Most adult books may be borrowed for 21 calendar days. New releases and high-demand items may have a 7-day loan period when the catalogue record or item label says so. The due date is printed or displayed at checkout. The loan period begins on the checkout date, not the date the patron first reads the book.

For exceptions or account-specific decisions, contact the Fairview Public Library circulation desk. This policy document is part of the fictional course dataset.
