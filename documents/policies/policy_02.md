# Policy: Renewal rules

Document ID: POL-02

Eligible books may be renewed once for an additional 14 calendar days. Renewal is not allowed when another patron has placed a hold, the item is already overdue beyond the renewal limit, or the account has a borrowing block. A renewal is complete only after the new due date is displayed or confirmed by staff.

For exceptions or account-specific decisions, contact the Fairview Public Library circulation desk. This policy document is part of the fictional course dataset.
