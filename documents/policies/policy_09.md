# Policy: Account blocks

Document ID: POL-09

An account may be temporarily blocked when replacement charges exceed PKR 1,000, required contact details need verification, or a staff review is pending. A block prevents new checkouts and may prevent renewals. Returning an item does not automatically remove every type of block; ask circulation staff to review the account.

For exceptions or account-specific decisions, contact the Fairview Public Library circulation desk. This policy document is part of the fictional course dataset.
