# Policy: High-demand items

Document ID: POL-12

High-demand items may have a 7-day loan period and may not be renewable when other patrons are waiting. The item record or checkout receipt identifies the applicable loan period. Do not infer the loan period from the book’s genre or publication date.

For exceptions or account-specific decisions, contact the Fairview Public Library circulation desk. This policy document is part of the fictional course dataset.
