# Policy: Borrowing limits

Document ID: POL-08

An adult card may borrow up to 8 circulating items at one time. A youth card may borrow up to 5 items. Reference-only materials do not count toward the circulating limit because they cannot be checked out. The limit applies across all eligible items, not separately to each genre.

For exceptions or account-specific decisions, contact the Fairview Public Library circulation desk. This policy document is part of the fictional course dataset.
