# Policy: Placing a hold

Document ID: POL-05

A patron with an active library card may request a hold on a checked-out or eligible circulating item. The library notifies the patron when the item is ready. A held item is kept at the pickup desk for three calendar days after notification. Holds are not confirmed until the catalogue or staff records the request.

For exceptions or account-specific decisions, contact the Fairview Public Library circulation desk. This policy document is part of the fictional course dataset.
