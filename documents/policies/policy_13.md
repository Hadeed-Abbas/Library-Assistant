# Policy: Interlibrary loan

Document ID: POL-13

A patron may ask staff whether a book held by another library can be requested through interlibrary loan. Availability, delivery time, renewal rules, and fees depend on the lending institution. The chat assistant cannot confirm a request that has not been submitted by staff.

For exceptions or account-specific decisions, contact the Fairview Public Library circulation desk. This policy document is part of the fictional course dataset.
