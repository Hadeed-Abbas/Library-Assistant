# Policy: Lost or damaged materials

Document ID: POL-04

Report lost or damaged materials to the circulation desk. Staff assess replacement charges using the item record and applicable library schedule. Do not assume the standard overdue fine is the full replacement charge. Patrons should not attempt to repair damaged books with tape or glue.

For exceptions or account-specific decisions, contact the Fairview Public Library circulation desk. This policy document is part of the fictional course dataset.
