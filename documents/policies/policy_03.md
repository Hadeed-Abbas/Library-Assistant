# Policy: Overdue fines

Document ID: POL-03

Standard overdue items incur a fictional fine of PKR 20 per item per calendar day, capped at PKR 600 per item. The fine stops accumulating when the item is returned or renewed if eligible. Lost or damaged items are handled separately and are not covered by the daily fine cap.

For exceptions or account-specific decisions, contact the Fairview Public Library circulation desk. This policy document is part of the fictional course dataset.
