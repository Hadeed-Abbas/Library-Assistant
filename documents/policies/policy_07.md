# Policy: Library cards and eligibility

Document ID: POL-07

Residents aged 16 or older may apply for an adult card with proof of identity and current address. Applicants under 16 need a parent or guardian to complete the application. Temporary visitors should ask staff about visitor access. The library does not ask patrons to share card numbers in the chat.

For exceptions or account-specific decisions, contact the Fairview Public Library circulation desk. This policy document is part of the fictional course dataset.
