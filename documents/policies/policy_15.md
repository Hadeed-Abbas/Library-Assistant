# Policy: Privacy and patron records

Document ID: POL-15

Patron borrowing records are used for library operations and should be accessed only by authorised staff. Do not post library card numbers, passwords, or identity document numbers in the chat. The demo assistant has no authenticated connection to live patron accounts and cannot reveal an individual’s private borrowing history.

For exceptions or account-specific decisions, contact the Fairview Public Library circulation desk. This policy document is part of the fictional course dataset.
