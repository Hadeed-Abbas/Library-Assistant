# Service guide: Lost property

Document ID: SVC-11

Found items should be handed to the service desk rather than kept or posted publicly. The library stores ordinary lost property for 30 days, after which unclaimed items are handled under the branch procedure. Do not include personal identifying details in a public chat when describing a lost item.

This guide describes the fictional Fairview Public Library used for the assignment demonstration. For live changes or account-specific requests, contact staff.
