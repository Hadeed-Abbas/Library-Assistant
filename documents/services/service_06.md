# Service guide: Printing and photocopying

Document ID: SVC-06

Black-and-white printing costs PKR 10 per page and colour printing costs PKR 30 per page. Photocopying is PKR 8 per black-and-white page. Prices apply to standard A4 pages and may differ for special formats. Payment is made at the service desk; the assistant cannot process payments.

This guide describes the fictional Fairview Public Library used for the assignment demonstration. For live changes or account-specific requests, contact staff.
