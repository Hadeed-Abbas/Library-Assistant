# Service guide: Digital resources

Document ID: SVC-13

Cardholders can use selected digital resources from the library website after signing in with their library credentials. The assistant cannot log in, reset passwords, or verify a patron’s subscription status. Patrons should use the official account recovery process and never share their password in chat.

This guide describes the fictional Fairview Public Library used for the assignment demonstration. For live changes or account-specific requests, contact staff.
