# Service guide: Donations

Document ID: SVC-09

The library accepts clean books in good condition subject to space and collection needs. Encyclopaedias, damaged books, mouldy items, and outdated test-preparation manuals are generally not accepted. Donated items become library property and may be added, sold, recycled, or declined at staff discretion.

This guide describes the fictional Fairview Public Library used for the assignment demonstration. For live changes or account-specific requests, contact staff.
