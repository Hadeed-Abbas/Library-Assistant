# Service guide: School visits

Document ID: SVC-14

Teachers may request a group visit by contacting the service desk at least two weeks in advance. The request should include the proposed date, group size, age range, and learning objective. A visit is not confirmed until staff reply. School groups must follow staff instructions while inside the branch.

This guide describes the fictional Fairview Public Library used for the assignment demonstration. For live changes or account-specific requests, contact staff.
