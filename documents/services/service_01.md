# Service guide: Opening hours

Document ID: SVC-01

The Fairview Public Library main branch is open Monday through Thursday from 9:00 AM to 7:00 PM, Friday from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM, and Saturday from 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM. The branch is closed on Sundays and listed public holidays. Hours can change for training or maintenance; check the notice at the entrance for same-day changes.

This guide describes the fictional Fairview Public Library used for the assignment demonstration. For live changes or account-specific requests, contact staff.
