# Service guide: Emergency and evacuation

Document ID: SVC-15

If an alarm sounds, stop using equipment and follow staff instructions to the nearest safe exit. Do not use lifts during an evacuation. Assemble at the marked meeting point outside the main entrance and do not re-enter until staff or emergency responders say it is safe.

This guide describes the fictional Fairview Public Library used for the assignment demonstration. For live changes or account-specific requests, contact staff.
