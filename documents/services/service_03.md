# Service guide: Study rooms

Document ID: SVC-03

The library has four study rooms that may be booked by cardholders aged 16 or older. A booking is limited to two hours per day per cardholder. Rooms must be vacated at the end of the reservation. Food is not allowed; covered drinks are permitted. The assistant can explain the policy but does not make a real reservation.

This guide describes the fictional Fairview Public Library used for the assignment demonstration. For live changes or account-specific requests, contact staff.
