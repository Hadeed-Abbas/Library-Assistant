# Service guide: Accessibility services

Document ID: SVC-07

The main branch has step-free entry, an accessible washroom, adjustable-height computer station, and large-print materials. Patrons who need help retrieving items from high shelves can ask staff. Contact the service desk before visiting to confirm availability of a specific accommodation.

This guide describes the fictional Fairview Public Library used for the assignment demonstration. For live changes or account-specific requests, contact staff.
